# Absolute UI/UX Perfection

## Workflow
1. Read the existing shared components, design tokens and the affected page content. Preserve brand recognition, local assets and established product boundaries.
2. Audit layout and copy together: page purpose, visitor question, hierarchy, readable line length, working next step, load/error/empty states and interaction feedback.
3. Refine with an 8px spacing grid and 4px micro-adjustments, stable dimensions, semantic colors, visible focus, 44px touch targets and 150–300ms transitions. Use fixed type sizes at content breakpoints rather than continuously scaling text. Respect reduced motion and transparency.
4. Reuse shared components. Keep page content in its page and common behavior in the shared system. Resizable surfaces must preserve readable page widths and usable input fields, support keyboard control, and not overlap.
5. Use existing transparent artwork unchanged. Add coherent rim light and contact shadows through semantic tokens. Glass combines a subtle tint, highlight, inner edge, soft depth and actual backdrop blur. Keep body text on legible surfaces and provide an opaque fallback.
6. Apply the editorial workflow below to all affected marketing, product, offer, pricing and legal material.
7. Verify real interaction flows on desktop and phone. Inspect screenshots, keyboard navigation, long copy, loading, errors, zoom and overflow. Run relevant tests and check the latest compilation result. Never claim full legal compliance or browser support from a partial check.

## Editorial workflow
- Identify each page's role and reader intent. Separate independent products, commissioned agency work, knowledge pages and legal disclosures.
- Use direct natural German: concrete headings, specific verbs, short paragraphs and useful examples. Remove interchangeable superlatives, filler, repeated pitch sections and generic AI-style language. Do not conceal that an assistant is AI.
- Lead product copy with what it does, who it fits, prerequisites, limitations, cost mechanism and the accurate next step. Keep Suite and API independent from agency work unless the published access conditions expressly require a project.
- Improve offer and pricing presentation without inventing tariffs, discounts, inclusions or guarantees. Separate one-time implementation, optional support and usage/external costs. State pending rates plainly.
- Use only verified project/public facts for legal information. Preserve actual provider details and draft status. Never fabricate retention periods, legal bases, processors, certification or legal review. Clearly distinguish application behavior from provider processing.
- Set descriptive unique page titles/descriptions and matching social metadata. Use meaningful internal links and natural topic terms, not keyword stuffing. Research search demand only before proposing new SEO topics; do not claim rankings or traffic gains without evidence.
- Check consistency across page body, navigation, repeated teasers, chat grounding and metadata. Update the affected public advisory catalog when a product fact changes.

## Delivery boundaries
Do not change secrets, access permissions, actual prices or contract terms as a visual/copy improvement. Report unknown business/legal facts as blockers; polished prose is not approval. State exactly what was verified and what remains unverified.
